Full Bio · ~120 words

Zuna Grey is a 4-piece alt-rock band from the Alberta sector of Canada, formed from the wreckage of prior projects (Candywheel, Lost Republik, Moll Buzzer, Hotel Breakfast). Their sound lands between post-grunge urgency and industrial dread — Radiohead's paranoia, Pixies' dynamics, Tool's patience, NIN's machine. Live, it's loud where it needs to be and quiet where it hurts more. Songs aren't sung so much as broadcast — warnings for anyone still listening.
